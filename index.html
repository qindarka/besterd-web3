What we do

Mechanical services, end to end.

From a single washroom to long-term, large-scale industrial installs — our trades cover the full mechanical scope, in-house.

Process & Power Piping

Welded and threaded process piping for manufacturing, food & beverage, and industrial facilities — coded welders and procedures.

  • Stainless steel schedule pipe, fittings & valves
  • Stainless sanitary tubing systems
  • Power piping & piping repairs
  • Underground Riewil piping systems

Plumbing

Commercial and industrial plumbing — from a single washroom rough-in to complete fit-ups for new builds and renovations.

  • Domestic water, sanitary & storm
  • Backflow preventer install & testing
  • Fixture rough-in & finish
  • Water heaters & recirculation

HVAC & Cooling

Engineered heating, ventilation, and cooling for new builds, retrofits, and high-performance upgrades.

  • Chiller installations
  • Chilled & condenser water piping
  • Make-up air & exhaust
  • Building automation integration

Steam & Boilers

Steam systems for industrial process work, district plants, and high-load institutional facilities.

  • Steam boiler installations
  • Steam & condensate piping
  • De-aerators
  • Heat-recovery integration

Natural & Medical Gas

Certified natural-gas and medical-gas piping for hospitals, labs, manufacturing, and commercial fit-ups.

  • Natural gas piping & distribution
  • Medical gas piping (CSA Z7396 standards)
  • Compressed air & specialty gases
  • Testing, certification & documentation

Radiant & Heating Systems

In-floor and in-ceiling radiant heating for warehouses, institutional facilities, and high-comfort commercial spaces.

  • Floor & ceiling radiant systems
  • Snow-melt & de-icing
  • Process heating loops
  • Hot water distribution

Design-Build

Single-source delivery: project managers, engineers, and trades partner with you from concept to commissioning — fixed-cost pricing available.

  • Mechanical system design
  • Constructability & budget planning
  • Permits, drawings & closeout
  • Infrastructure Ontario projects

Service & Maintenance

A dedicated service truck running London and area daily — preventive maintenance and rapid-response repair for institutional and commercial clients.

  • Preventive maintenance programs
  • Troubleshooting & diagnostics
  • Equipment replacement
  • 24/7 emergency dispatch

Sewage & Water Treatment

Mechanical work for municipal and private sewage and water treatment plants — built to handle harsh service conditions.

  • Treatment plant piping & equipment
  • Pump & lift station work
  • Specialty alloys for corrosive service
  • Coordinated shutdown planning

About Besterd

A London, Ontario name you can build on.

Founded in London in 1961 by William Besterd, our company has spent more than six decades earning the trust of institutional, commercial, and industrial clients across Southwestern Ontario. Today we’re still focused on what got us here: professionalism, integrity, and quality workmanship.

Our crews combine traditional craftsmanship with modern tools, methods, and safety practices. Whether you’re planning a ground-up build, a system upgrade, or just need a problem solved fast, you’ll work with people who treat your project like it’s their own.

  • Fully licensed & insured. Plumbers, gas-fitters, refrigeration mechanics, and welders — certified to current Ontario standards and affiliated with UA Local 593.
  • Safety first, always. Site-specific safety plans and ongoing training keep our people, your people, and your facility protected.
  • Single point of accountability. Dedicated project managers from quote through commissioning — no hand-offs, no surprises.
  • Built to last. Quality materials, clean installs, and detailed closeout packages so you’ll thank us in year ten as much as on day one.
